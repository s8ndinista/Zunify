# Upstream and assets

- [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast), MIT, at `b75af93ad5b157ab4205710be2f0ea7878587082`. This port reimplements the main listening flows for FerroUI and Spotify's browser APIs. The upstream MIT notice is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- [FerroUI](https://github.com/wieslawsoltes/FerroUI), MIT, pinned to `e1b36bb743a44277d27d63aa461ee671e54e9ec5` from `main`. The framework and bundled font notices are in the submodule.
- [librespot 0.8.0](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/tree/v0.8.0) provides native Spotify playback under its [MIT license](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/blob/v0.8.0/LICENSE).
- [Lofty](https://github.com/Serial-ATA/lofty-rs) reads desktop audio tags under MIT or Apache 2.0. [music-metadata](https://github.com/Borewit/music-metadata) reads browser audio tags under MIT.
